Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

In short: Blushwell has no accounts, no servers and no ads, and we run no analytics. We do not collect your data. Your photo, and everything the app reads from it, stays on your phone.

Your photo and your face

When you take a selfie or choose a photo, Blushwell finds the features of your face (the outline, eyes, brows, nose and lips) with software that runs on your phone: Apple's Vision framework on iPhone, and Google's ML Kit, bundled inside the app, on Android. From those points it estimates your face shape, eye shape, lip fullness, skin depth and undertone. The photo and these results are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and are left out of cloud backups. They are never uploaded, and we cannot see them. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Delete My Face, or everything with Delete Everything; deleting the app removes them too.

The app does not identify who you are, does not compare your face with anyone else's, and uses your face only to draw the make-up map and suggest colours.

The mirror

The mirror shows the camera's live picture with the map on top. Each frame is analysed on the phone and then thrown away. Nothing is recorded or saved, and the camera stops when you leave the mirror or the app.

Everything else you enter

Your make-up bag, the looks you build, notes and the history of looks you finished are stored only on your phone.

The coach

Questions you ask the coach are answered on your phone: from the app's own library and, on iPhones that support it, by Apple's on-device language model. Questions are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Reminders

If you turn on the practice reminder, the notification is scheduled by your phone itself. No server is involved.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive your payment details. Their own privacy policies apply to purchases.

Permissions

Camera: to take a selfie and for the mirror. Photos: only the picture you pick in the system photo picker. Notifications: only if you turn on the reminder.

Children

Blushwell does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
